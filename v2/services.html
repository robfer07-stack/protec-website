01 — Our speciality

AOX full-arch restorations

All-on-X (AOX) restorations are at the heart of what we do. Working within your workflow, we adapt to produce the full-arch restorations your patients need — from immediate-load provisionals to definitive prostheses. We work with all major implant companies and keep up to date with the latest innovations.

Every AOX case is planned around the final outcome: implant positions are captured accurately, the design is shared for your approval, and the final prosthesis is milled in-house and finished by hand.

What’s included

  • Screw-retained full-arch prostheses on four or more implants

  • Prototype or try-in stage before the final [confirm]
  • Monolithic or layered zirconia finals [confirm materials]

  • Photogrammetry-verified implant positions

02 — Digital implant capture

Photogrammetry

Photogrammetry has long been the benchmark for capturing digital implant positions. At your request, we can facilitate several PIC and iMetric cameras for your cases — giving the accuracy full-arch work depends on, without conventional splinted impressions.

What’s included

  • PIC camera capture
  • iMetric capture
  • Data merged with intraoral scans for design

03 — Plan before you place

Case planning & smile design

We understand how important case planning is to ensure the end result is as close to plan as possible. We can provide a smile design as well as other planning paths, so you and your patient can see the destination before treatment begins.

What’s included

  • Design previews for patient communication

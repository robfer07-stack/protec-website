02 / FULL ARCH

Complex full-arch cases are what we do best.

From simple cases to extremely complex restorations, clinicians rely on our experience with AOX workflows. We adapt to the way you work and keep up with the latest innovations from the major implant companies.

  • Digital implant position capture with photogrammetry (PIC & iMetric)
  • Case planning and smile design before the final is made
  • Try-in / prototype stage to confirm aesthetics, function and fit [confirm]
  • Final prosthesis milled in-house and hand-finished
How our AOX service works

  1. Records & capture

    Intraoral scans, photos, bite and photogrammetry data.

  2. Plan & design

    Smile design and prosthetic design, shared for your approval.

  3. Prototype & try-in

    Verify aesthetics, phonetics and occlusion with the patient.

  4. Mill & finish

    Final prosthesis milled, characterised and quality-checked.

04 / For clinicians

Sending a case is simple

Digital or analogue, we fit around the workflow you already have.

  1. Scan or capture

    Send an intraoral scan, photogrammetry file or physical impressions — whatever suits your clinic.

  2. Send the case

    Use our online case form or your scanner’s lab portal, with your Rx and any photos.

  3. Review the design

    We share the digital design for your approval before anything is milled.

  4. Milled, finished, delivered

    Your restoration is milled in-house, hand-finished, checked and returned.

Our story

Small beginnings, big capability

ProTec Dental Laboratory started as a small, boutique lab and has grown into a full-service hub for complex restorative cases.

We’ve scaled our technology and expertise to take on everything from full-arch rehabilitations to intricate digital workflows. But our core hasn’t changed: a commitment to precision craftsmanship and the personal touch of a local partner.
